FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply my own Gemini API Key?

You can apply your Gemini API key in two places:
1. In this Web App: Click the Cloud Mode / Custom Key button in the top navigation bar, paste your key, and tap Verify & Save.
2. In the Android App: Tap the Settings ⚙️ icon in the top right, paste your Google AI Studio API key, and hit Save. Your key is stored exclusively on your device and enables unlimited high-speed requests.

Can Apple iPhone & iPad users install EchoBand?

Yes! Apple users can download the Apple iOS WebClip Profile (.mobileconfig) directly from this page, or open Safari and tap Share > Add to Home Screen. It installs an app icon on your iPhone home screen that launches full-screen with offline caching.

Why does Android show a warning when installing the APK?

Android displays a standard "File might be harmful" or "Unknown app" warning whenever an APK is installed directly from the web rather than the Google Play Store. VEXLORA EchoBand is 100% open-source, safe, and contains zero trackers or ads. You can safely tap Install anyway.

Does it only work with Sony WH-CH510 headphones?

While EchoBand is specifically tuned for Sony WH-CH510 button hooks and Bluetooth SCO profiles, it works seamlessly with any Bluetooth earbuds, wired headphones, or directly through your phone's speaker and microphone.
